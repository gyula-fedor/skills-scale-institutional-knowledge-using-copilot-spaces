# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with Product and Development teams to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy and test plans aligned with feature scope
- Create and maintain test cases and test documentation
- Perform manual and automated testing
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before release
- Identify and document quality issues with severity assessment
- Collaborate on Definition of Done standards

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects in production
- Reduce cycle time through efficient testing practices
- Enable continuous delivery with confidence

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions during sprint planning
- Daily standups and status updates
- Quality reports and release readiness assessments
- Incident investigations and root cause analysis

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Developers**: QA/Testing Leads work closely with Developers during implementation to clarify acceptance criteria and coordinate testing strategies. They review code for testability and provide feedback on quality issues.
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria definition and success metrics to ensure quality standards align with product goals.
- **Project Managers**: Provide quality status updates and release readiness assessments to inform project timelines and risk management.

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own technical design decisions, system architecture, and technical risk assessment. They provide technical guidance and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture for projects
- Review technical designs and code for architectural alignment
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Ensure technical documentation is current and accessible
- Collaborate with other technical leads across teams on shared systems

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and system complexity
- Enable team velocity through good architectural decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical spike investigations
- Risk register updates for technical concerns
- Code review and mentoring

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Developers**: Technical Leads mentor Developers on architecture decisions and review designs to ensure alignment with technical strategy.
- **Product Managers**: Partner on feasibility assessments and trade-offs between feature scope and technical constraints.
- **Project Managers**: Highlight technical risks and dependencies that impact project schedules and resource planning.

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches facilitate team ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on agile practices. They act as process guardians and enable continuous improvement in delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, retrospectives, and reviews
- Identify and help remove impediments blocking team progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Maintain sprint boards and ensure process compliance
- Facilitate communication and collaboration within the team
- Track and report on team health metrics and process improvements

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Enable self-organizing teams to work effectively
- Remove organizational blockers to team success

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching sessions
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Team health and velocity metrics

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Developers**: Scrum Masters support Developers by removing impediments and facilitating collaboration.
- **Product Managers**: Partner on backlog refinement and sprint planning to ensure clear priorities and scope.
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on cross-team dependencies and escalations that impact delivery.

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors represent business interests, provide funding and approval authority, and escalate business-level risks. They ensure projects align with strategic business objectives and have necessary resources.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria for initiatives
- Provide funding and resource approval for projects
- Communicate strategic context and business priorities
- Make business-level trade-off decisions
- Escalate business risks and constraints
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between project outcomes and strategic objectives
- Mitigate business risks and dependencies
- Optimize return on investment

### Typical Communication
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Strategic alignment reviews and prioritization sessions
- Business case and ROI assessments
- Risk escalation and decision authority

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Product Managers**: Set business priorities and define success metrics that guide product direction.
- **Project Managers**: Provide escalation authority, funding decisions, and business context for trade-offs.
- **Technical Leads**: Communicate business constraints and strategic context that inform technical decision-making.

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience strategy, conduct user research, and ensure design consistency. They advocate for users and translate research insights into design requirements that guide product development.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Define information architecture and user workflows
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Ensure design consistency across products and features
- Collaborate with Product and Development teams on implementation
- Advocate for user needs in product discussions

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Build customer loyalty through superior UX
- Enable data-informed design decisions

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and insights sessions
- Design reviews and feedback on prototypes
- Design systems and component documentation
- Usability testing reports and recommendations

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Product Managers**: Partner on user research, feature prioritization, and success metrics tied to user satisfaction.
- **Developers**: Provide detailed design specifications and collaborate on implementation to ensure pixel-perfect delivery.
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Work together to define usability acceptance criteria and conduct user acceptance testing.

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure security requirements are met and compliance standards are followed. They identify security risks, guide secure practices, and ensure adherence to regulatory and organizational requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and standards for projects
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Identify compliance requirements and audit obligations
- Guide secure development practices and code review
- Manage security incidents and vulnerabilities
- Report on security and compliance metrics

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data loss
- Ensure organizational compliance with regulations
- Build security into development processes from the start
- Maintain stakeholder confidence in security posture

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat modeling sessions
- Compliance audit and assessment reporting
- Vulnerability disclosure and incident response
- Security awareness and training communications

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Developers**: Provide secure coding guidance and review code for security vulnerabilities.
- **Technical Leads**: Consult on architectural security decisions and threat mitigation strategies.
- **Project Managers**: Highlight security and compliance risks that impact project scope and timeline.
- **Stakeholders**: Report on compliance status and security posture to satisfy audit and governance requirements.
